    What the full evaluation measured

    Nine scenarios, five unseen traffic seeds, three training runs per setting; every controller saw identical vehicles.

    • The trained agents spend about 24% less time in the system than a tuned fixed timer and about 8% less than a guarded max-pressure controller, and are ahead of both in all nine scenarios.
    • Emergency vehicles stop about 0.2 times per trip under the agents, against about 2 under either baseline.
    • The cooperative reward did not beat the selfish one: selfish agents are about 2% better.
    • No green wave beyond a synchronised fixed timer was measured: both score about 0.55 on the arterial Green Wave Score.
    • Disruptions cost the agents about as much extra queueing as they cost the fixed timer; the agents stay ahead, they do not recover faster.
